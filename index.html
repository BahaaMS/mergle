      How to Play

      Every day, two real countries that actually share a border are fused into one silhouette. Guess both of them in 6 attempts.

      Guessing

      Type a country into either box — or both at once — and hit Submit. Whether you fill one box or two, it counts as a single attempt.

      Reading the feedback

      Guess correctly and it locks in green right away. Guess wrong and you get a colored arrow pointing toward the real country plus the distance to it. Both are measured between the two countries’ closest border points, not their centers — cool white means far off, deep red means very close, and the darkest red means you named one of its actual neighbors. If both boxes are wrong in the same attempt, only the closer guess reveals its arrow and distance; the other just tells you it lost.

      Hints

      Reveal half border sweeps out part of the actual shared border between the two countries (a second click reveals the rest). Show neighbor outlines traces the surrounding countries for context. Both are optional — skipping them keeps your hint-free streak in Stats.

      Example

      Say today's fused shape is secretly Ecuador + Peru:

      Attempt 1
      ↖ Argentina 554 km

      Wrong, but close — Peru is about 550 km to the northwest, and the deep red badge says you’re near.

      Attempt 2
      Peru Correct ✓ ↖ Bolivia 1,216 km

      Peru locks in green. Bolivia was wrong too, but since Peru is already solved, its distance is measured to the only country still left to find — Ecuador.

      Attempt 3
      Ecuador Correct ✓

      Both found — solved in 3 of 6 attempts, no hints used.
